Privacy Policy

Chargi Blach is a free rewards game. This policy explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account: the username and password you choose. Passwords are stored encrypted (hashed); we cannot read them.
  • Device identifier: an anonymous ID from your phone, used only to allow one account per phone and prevent abuse.
  • Phone number (optional): only if you add it, and only so we can contact you if you win a prize. It is never shown to other players.
  • Gameplay data: points, check-ins, games played and your ranking.
  • Ads: we show ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers to serve ads. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

How we use it

  • To run the game, keep your points and ranking, and pick and contact weekly winners.
  • To prevent cheating and multiple accounts.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Your choices

  • You can remove or change your phone number in the app at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data by contacting us (below).
  • You can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Children

This app is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at young children.

Contest Rules

  • Chargi Blach is free to play. You never pay to join or to win.
  • Each week, one winner is chosen from the top of the ranking and receives the announced prize.
  • You must have a valid contact (phone number) to receive a prize.
  • Any account using cheating, bots or multiple accounts may be removed without a prize.
  • This contest is run by Chargi Blach only. It is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Google or Apple.
